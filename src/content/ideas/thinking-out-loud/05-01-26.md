I have this terrible habit of opening my editor only to be met with an overwhelming sense of disgust at a project I have quite frankly invested too much time in. As a result I often find myself throwing away weeks or even months of progress on a whim. There is immense satisfaction that comes from highlighting project files and pressing shift+delete to permanently erase them. It's cathartic and akin to burning a cursed artifact in purifying flame.

The urge to throw everything away and restart is so ingrained in me that I have no idea how to prevent it long term. Git and github were my original solution. If I can't stop myself from deleting progress then I suppose I ought to protect my myself from the consequences. The only problem with this is that I simply don't commit enough for fear of adding garbage to the canonical project history.

My plan this year is to become more comfortable with garbage. To be comfortable sitting in, working in, and playing in the messy projects. Even now I am tempted to read back this very post - to nit pick and editorialize until I'm *"satisfied"*. It's a feeling I'm familiar with and I know enough now to understand that the only road it leads down is anti-progress. 

In an act of rebellion against the very fiber of my being I intend to *"complete"* this post and simply *send* it without pomp or circumstance.

> Nothing is ever finished, it is only shipped.
